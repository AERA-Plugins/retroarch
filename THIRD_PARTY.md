# Third-party software

The binary payload contains RetroArch (GPLv3), Gambatte (GPLv2), libretro-2048
(public domain), retroarch-assets (CC BY 4.0), common-overlays, and musl libc. Source revisions
and the complete AERA adaptation are documented in this repository. AERA does
not bundle ROMs, firmware, or BIOS images.
